Why these

The rule the palette enforces

One kind of thing on screen, at every depth

The predecessor drew machine-derived clusters as circles, then drilled from those circles into real resources and real edges. Two ontologies, one visual grammar, and a drill gesture promising “same thing, closer.” Two readers detected the discontinuity and had nowhere to put it but themselves — “what am I looking at”, and “why when I click this circle does it navigate into a graph, were we not in a graph before?”

There is no second kind to change into. A node is a resource; an edge is a row in kb_edges. Violating that requires adding a mark type, which is a visible act someone has to review.

Groupings still decide what appears — they are how the question gets answered at all. They simply never get drawn. The right-hand panel is the only place they exist, it is written without the words region, salience or survey, and it is magenta so you can confirm at a glance that none of it leaked onto the chart.

Always on screen · never dismissible

Three ways this view is incomplete, not one

The bound line under the chart is chrome, not a warning. It shows whether the view is complete or partial, so complete is something you are told rather than something you infer from an absence of alarms.

Notice what the line does not say. Only one of the three has a real x of y, and it is the one nothing downstream measures — how many of your places were asked, which the surface knows because it chose them. The other two have no denominator anywhere.

That is not an omission to fix later. Asked how many results a walk really had, the substrate answers absent rather than guessed from the page size — it returns a page, not a count, and declines to imply otherwise. A surface that printed “50 of 314” would be inventing the number its own foundation refused to invent. So the walk says more exist, or it says complete, and those are the only two honest things it can say.

The same restraint governs the ranking. Groupings come back with a blended score that runs slightly negative at one end and past 1.0 at the other, and whether that blend is even the right question is still an open ruling. So the panel puts them in order and never prints the number.

Four beats

  1. The plan builder and the bound line

    Pure modules, fully tested, no rendering. Every machine-checkable promise in the goal is proven here, against no UI at all.

  2. The surface

    Four URL parameters, three entries, one mark vocabulary, the why-these panel. Built on the layout code that survives.

  3. Somewhere for the analysis to live

    Charter, staleness and the regulating concepts get a place that says outright it is analysis. Thirteen months of measurements with no reader.

  4. Delete the old marks

    Last, so nothing is ever displaced into nowhere.
